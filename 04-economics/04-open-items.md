## The three questions that would most change the model

1. **Is a defunct ski area "farm land" for Overseas Investment Act purposes?** → New Zealand
   counsel. Worth NZ$100–250k/yr and the difference between one business and four.
2. **How does Chile's SII assess non-arable cordillera terrain?** → free *avalúo* certificate.
   If bare rock and scree carry near-zero productive class, Chilean holding cost is close to
   nil and Chile wins the study outright on carry.
3. **What does ski liability insurance actually cost in each jurisdiction?** → broker. The
   NZ/US delta alone was estimated at US$0.8–5.6 m over a 20-year hold.
