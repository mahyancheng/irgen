# Japan — private vendors, and what survives a private purchase

MYR at ¥1 = 0.02540.

---

## 1. ⚠️ The blocker nobody flagged, and it changes the diligence order

> ### Under 自然公園法, in a 特別保護地区 of a National or Quasi-National Park, **snowmobiles and all vehicles are banned across the entire zone** — plus 車馬等乗入れ規制地区 inside ordinary 特別地域.
>
> **Penalty: six months' imprisonment or a ¥500,000 fine.**
>
> **And it applies on privately owned land.** 自然公園 is a **地域制公園** — a zoning overlay, not a
> land holding. **Freehold buys no exemption whatsoever.** `[P1 環境省]`

**Every region named in this study's Japanese brief is substantially inside a park**: 八幡平, 栗駒,
蔵王, 鳥海, 月山, 妙高, 志賀, 白馬, 乗鞍, 木曽御嶽.

> **Sequencing conclusion: screen park status *before* tenure diligence. It is free, and it fails
> faster.** Ordering a title certificate on a parcel inside a 特別保護地区 is wasted money.

### But two obstacles survive, and they bind

**(a) Japanese ski slopes usually sit on land the operator does not own** — 民法上の賃借権 (not
借地借家法-protected), 国有林 permits, or 財産区 / 入会 commons. **A private vendor cannot sell what
it does not hold.**

> ⚠️ **The worked example is chilling: チャオ御岳 (Gifu) ran on ~54 ha of *leased national forest*.
> The lease lapsed in March 2022, and the state now demands 原状回復, 返地 — and damages for
> unauthorised occupation.** `[P2 岐阜新聞]`
>
> **That is precisely the liability this study has been warning about — and the company was
> privately owned and freely traded twice.** A private vendor is no protection against a public
> landlord underneath.

**(b) Summits and ridges are the least likely part of any Japanese mountain to be private.**
国有林 is 31 % of forest nationally and the Forestry Agency states it is concentrated in **奥地脊梁
山地 — remote spine-ridge terrain — and water-source areas.** And forest boundaries are drawn
**along** ridgelines: **a ridge is typically the boundary *between* holdings, not the interior of
one.** `[P1/E]`

Industry sources put it bluntly: **「山全体を買うことはできません」** — *you cannot buy a whole
mountain.* You buy a parcel.

> **300 m of vertical is obtainable. 300 m ending at a titled summit is the rare part.**

| Niigata (as at 31 Mar 2026) | ¥1,266/坪 ≈ ¥3.83 m | RM 97,282 |

> **A 40 ha forest block is ¥2–38 m — RM 50,800 to RM 965,200. The one ex-ski-area candidate with
> a price is asking ¥380 m (RM 9.65 m) for 13 ha.**
>
> **Roughly 58 % of Japanese forest is private, and about 80 % of sellers are individuals.** This is
> a deep, liquid, low-priced market — and it is the opposite of the ski-area market in every
> respect.

### ⚠️ Correction: 保安林 is a feature, not a bug

This study previously treated **保安林 (protection forest) designation as a serious negative.** For
this client that is wrong.

> **Sale and transfer of ownership are entirely unrestricted on 保安林.** What is restricted is
> **felling and land-form change** — neither of which a ski-touring buyer needs. **And the
> designation cuts the price.** `[P1/P2 — 山いちば]`
>
> **For a buyer who will never cut a tree or move earth, 保安林 is a discount on land he can use
> exactly as he intended.** It also carries the tax exemption already noted.

**Channels:** **山林売買.net is actively soliciting 100 ha+ blocks in Gifu and Nagano.** 山いちば
runs a **buy-side wanted board** — the client can post requirements rather than browse listings.

## 5. ⚠️ Three corrections to this study's own briefing

**(a) The 森林法 nationality field takes effect 令和8年4月1日 — 1 April 2026 — not July 2024.**
`[P1 林野庁]` Corporate filers also give the representative's nationality, and where a majority of
officers or voting rights are of one nationality, that country.

**(b) 国土利用計画法 事後届出 thresholds are three-tiered**, not one: **市街化区域 2,000 m² /
都市計画区域内 5,000 m² / 都市計画区域外 10,000 m².** The 10,000 m² figure applies only *outside* a
都市計画区域. `[P1 国交省]`

**(c) The water-source ordinances are much weaker than this study implied.** Three qualifications:

> 1. **In most prefectures the filing duty is on the SELLER, not the buyer.**
> 2. **They bite only inside a designated zone — and the zones are tiny. Nagano's entire designated
>    area is 567.25 ha across 19 areas in 6 municipalities.** A rounding error.
> 3. **None confers a veto.** The governor may advise, request reports and inspect. **That is
>    categorically different from a council vote.**

**The distinction to hold onto:** the *ordinance* is weak. **Water as a political argument is
not** — it is what a Tsunan councillor used to kill a signed deal. The legal gate is minor; the
political one is real.

## 6. ⏳ The clock is now specific

- **不動産登記 nationality disclosure becomes mandatory on 5 October 2026 — about five weeks from
  the report date** — on 所有権保存, 移転 and 更正登記, **for Japanese and foreigners alike.**
  `[P2 時事]`
- **The government adopted a foreign land-acquisition tightening package on 23 January 2026**; the
  expert panel convened 4 March 2026 and produces its 骨格 this summer, with an LDP–Ishin
  commitment to a bill. **Measures so far are disclosure-based, not prohibitive** — targeting
  foreigners alone risks treaty breach. `[P2 時事]`

> **If proceeding, completing before the framework is legislated is materially lower risk than
> completing after.**

## 7. ⚠️ The question this raises about the whole premise

**Whole-resort private charter already exists in Japan, and it is cheap.** `[P3]`

| Asset | Price |
|---|---|
| **ばんしゅう戸倉** — the **entire ski area**, 3 lifts, snowcat ride and snowmobile | **¥300,000/day** (**RM 7,620**) |
| **薬師スキー場** — whole area, no lifts | **¥15,000/day** (**RM 381**) |

> **Twenty days a season costs ¥0.3–6 m — RM 7,600 to RM 152,000 — against ¥380 m for 13 hectares,
> plus perpetual tax, compliance and demolition exposure.**
>
> **This deserves to be put to the client directly, not buried.** If the objective is *skiing a
> mountain privately with friends*, chartering achieves it for roughly the annual property tax on
> the alternative. If the objective is **owning land** — an asset, a base, something to build and
> pass on — then charter does not substitute, and the acquisition case stands on its own terms.
>
> **Those are different goals and the client should choose deliberately, not by default.**
