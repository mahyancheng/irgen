# Hokkaido farmsteads — and what Mountain Memories actually is

MYR at ¥1 = 0.02540.

---

## 1. ⚠️ The model is not what it appears to be

**Mountain Memories: 90 Rusutsu, Rusutsu-mura, Abuta-gun 048-1731** (北海道虻田郡留寿都村字留寿都90).
TripAdvisor classifies it as a **hostel**. The owners' own copy describes **"an old Japanese farm
transformed into a luxury space,"** transformed by **"Noah, Henry & Co"**; a guest review names
"Noah and Henry the owners"; operators cite **twelve seasons.** `[P3]`

> ### The premise is right about the building and wrong about the mountain.
>
> Their own description: **"a cozy cabin out in the farmland of Rusutsu," "about 5 minutes from
> the lifts," "next to Rusutsu Resort."**
>
> **There is no hill out the back.** It is a **lift-served resort lodge on the agricultural flats
> beside a 37-run commercial resort.** 尻別岳 is a drive away.
>
> **The product is hosted weeks, an in-house chef, shuttles and airport transfers — hospitality and
> curation, not private terrain.**

**Nothing was found on acquisition price, land area, 地目, or even whether they own the freehold.**
`[U]`

**Operationally** `[P3/E]`: dorms (4- and 3-person bunk rooms) plus two private rooms; states it
"operates as a licensed accommodation provider"; and **ski instruction is delivered through an
*affiliated* ski school — guiding is deliberately held in a separate entity.** A year-round dorm
lodge needs a **簡易宿所 licence under the 旅館業法** to escape the 180-night 民泊 cap; **fire and
building code compliance is the real cost.** Applications go to the **Shiribeshi 保健所**. `[P1]`

> **What is being copied here is a hospitality business next to a commercial ski resort. That is a
> perfectly good business — but it is not the private mountain the mandate has been chasing, and it
> would not require owning a hill at all.**

## 2. The structural finding: it has to be a two-parcel assembly

> **No live listing combines buildings + meaningful land + an owned hill with 300 m of vertical.**
>
> **That is structural, not a search failure.** Buildings sit on **宅地 on valley floors, where the
> road, power and water are.** The hills are **山林 / 原野, held separately** by the state, the
> prefecture, the municipality or forestry owners. `[E]`

**The model has to be built, not bought:** a farmstead parcel for the base, and — if the mountain
is to be owned at all — a separate forest parcel for the slope. **They will not come as one lot.**

---

## 3. The 農地法 wall — real, worse since April 2025, and avoidable

**Article 3 is closed to this client.** The 2023 abolition of the 下限面積要件 (2 ha in Hokkaido) is
**widely misread** — it was scrapped to let *small new farmers* in. The two tests that actually
block a foreign investor **survive intact**: **全部効率利用要件** (must efficiently farm *all* land
held) and **農作業常時従事要件** (~150 days/year of farm work). `[P1]`

**And it got harder.** MAFF amended the Enforcement Regulations **effective 1 April 2025**: foreign
applicants must declare **在留資格 and remaining period of stay**, and **short-term or unstable
status is refused.** Nationality of individuals and of directors and major shareholders is reported
to the Agriculture Committee; companies with >10 % foreign voting rights face special explanation
duties; foreign-bank borrowing above 50 % of equity triggers stricter review. Driven by a record
**175.3 ha acquired by foreign-related entities in 2024** — still 0.004 % of national farmland.
`[P1/P2]`

> **A Malaysia resident with no Japanese residence status at all is outside this gate entirely** —
> not merely likely to fail, but unable to satisfy the threshold requirement.

**The open door is wide, though:** **宅地 / 山林 / 原野 / 雑種地 are acquirable on identical terms
and identical documents as for a Japanese national.** The 外国人土地法 (1925) has had **no
designating ordinances since 1945 and is a dead letter**; the 2022 重要土地等調査法 covers defence
sites and border islands only. `[P1/P2]`

### The four workarounds, ranked

| # | Route | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Buy only non-farmland.** Screen 地目 before anything else | ✅ **Best** |
| **2** | **非農地証明 (non-farmland certificate).** Land **outside 農用地区域**, abandoned as a rule **20+ years**, now built-on, forested or self-seeded scrub, can be certified not-farmland and the register changed — **after which 農地法 stops applying.** Applied for by the **owner or successor — i.e. the seller, pre-completion** | ✅ **The clean route for a genuinely derelict ranch** |
| **3** | **農地法第5条 (transfer + conversion).** ⭐ **Crucially, Article 5 does not impose the 常時従事 test** — non-farmers, developers and construction firms use it routinely. Joint application by seller and buyer; governor-level permit | ✅ Viable |
| 4 | 農地付き空き家 municipal schemes | ❌ **Ignore.** They only lowered the *area* floor, which no longer exists; they never waived 常時従事; and they target resident in-migrants |

### ⚠️ But the actual killer is 農用地区域 (青地), not 地目

Land inside a **農用地区域** is reserved for agriculture for 10+ years, and **conversion applications
are rejected 100 % of the time while it remains 青地.** You must first win **農振除外** — a municipal
plan amendment needing prefectural approval and three cumulative tests (no alternative site, no
agricultural harm, 10+ years elapsed) — **explicitly harder than the conversion permit itself.**
`[P1]`

> **Check 農用地区域 status before 地目. It is the gate behind the gate.**

### And a point that cuts in the client's favour

**地目「牧場」 *is* caught by 農地法 — but the test is 現況主義, judged on actual present condition.**
Forest or moor actually being grazed **is** caught. **Registered 牧場 that is genuinely no longer
agricultural *can* be released via 非農地証明.** `[P1]`

> **On a truly abandoned ranch — which is exactly what the client is looking for — this works in
> their favour.**

## 6. Terrain, snow, and the back mountain

**Rusutsu settlement sits at 300–400 m**, normal maximum snow depth **~160 cm**, design snow load
230 cm. Kutchan's normal annual snowfall is **~17.7 m**, the highest of Japan's major JMA stations.
`[P1]`

| Mountain | Detail |
|---|---|
| **尻別岳 (1,107 m)** | The local backcountry **"mecca"** — about 8 routes, ~2 h up and ~20 min down. ⭐ **The forest road is unploughed in winter — so a snowcat or snowmobile is genuinely useful here, not a luxury** |
| **羊蹄山 (1,898 m)** | Makkari course, **~1,500 m of vertical**, 8 h up, 2 h down |

`[E]` **Shiribetsu gives roughly 700–800 m from a valley start — well past the 300 m target.** But
**no privately ownable 300 m hill exists in the Rusutsu bowl outside the resort's own footprint.**

### ⭐ And the back-mountain question has a working answer

The **後志総合振興局森林室 operates a live 入林承認証 system for 道有林 (prefectural forest)**: it
screens location, purpose and period, **issues approval by post in about a week**, and **explicitly
lists 森林散策 and 森林レクリエーション as valid purposes.** `[P1]`

Parcel-level ownership is resolvable from the Forestry Agency's 国有林 maps and **Hokkaido's 道有林
open data at 林班 / 小班 level.**

> **This substantially de-risks the whole "you don't need to own the mountain" premise** — at least
> for private recreation. Whether *commercial guiding* is treated the same way is the open question.
> `[E]`

## 7. What this means

**The farmstead model survives, but in a different shape than imagined:**

1. **Buy a clean base parcel** — 宅地 + 山林/原野, no 農地, outside 農用地区域. **The Ranshima
   akiya at ¥2.5 m and the two Rusutsu 山林/原野 parcels are the live templates.**
2. **Do not expect the hill to come with it.** Use 道有林 or 国有林 under 入林承認 for touring.
3. **Buy in the ring villages, not Niseko.** 留寿都, 真狩, 京極, 蘭越, 黒松内, 赤井川 — same snow, and
   **Hirafu is 38.6× the price.**
4. **If a hill must be owned, assemble a second parcel** — and screen it for park status *before*
   tenure, because a 特別保護地区 designation bans vehicles on private land outright.
