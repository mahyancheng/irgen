## The convergence

Two workstreams ran independently. One searched **terrain** — named hills with 250–400 m of relief,
outside park boundaries, in a snow belt. One searched **listings** — actual freehold parcels for
sale with a slope inside the boundary.

**They returned the same village.**

| | Terrain workstream | Listings workstream |
|---|---|---|
| Result | 赤井川村 — caldera, 575 m relief, outside every park | 赤井川村 山林, **74.4 ha, ¥25,000,000** |

That is the strongest signal this study has produced. It is not confirmation — both could be wrong
about different things — but the landform search and the market search arriving at one 
municipality of Hokkaido's 179 is worth taking seriously.

Three observations on that table:

1. **A private seller on a classifieds site, disposing of inherited land, is the ideal vendor
   profile** for this thesis. No agency, no marketing premium, no municipal 用途指定 or 買戻特約,
   no employment covenant — the entire apparatus that killed the municipal-transfer candidates.
2. **¥0 property tax most likely means 保安林** `[E]`. That cuts the price and the carrying cost —
   but see the qualification in `07-terrain-and-snow/05-sasa-clearing.md`: 保安林 is a discount for
   a buyer who tours untouched ground and a **constraint** for one who clears, because 立木の伐採
   and 土地の形質変更 are exactly the restricted acts.
3. **74.4 ha at MYR 635,000 is inside any plausible budget for this mandate**, with room left for
   plant, a building and the clearing programme.

## The one question that decides it

> **Nobody has established where in the village this parcel is.**
>
> The earlier "字富田" attribution **could not be corroborated by any source** and should be treated
> as unknown. Akaigawa is a caldera: the floor is flat farmland at ~150 m, the rim rises to 725 m.
> **A 74.4 ha parcel on the floor is flat and worthless for this purpose. The same parcel on the rim
> is the answer to the entire brief.**

This is answerable, and cheaply — the vendor is a private individual with a published mobile number.
**Obtain the 地番, then read the contours off 地理院地図.** That single step converts the leading
candidate from speculation to a measured proposition, and it requires no further web research.

## The market structure, which explains why nothing is perfect

The listings workstream's central finding: **nothing on the market meets all three requirements at
once**, and the reason is structural rather than bad luck.

| What you want | What the market offers |
|---|---|
| Ranch buildings | Comes attached to **1.5–5 ha** |
| 250 m+ of contiguous fall line | Comes attached to **74–365 ha of bare forest** |

**地目 is doing all the filtering.** Anything with ranch character carries 畑 or 田 → 農地法 blocks
a foreign passive buyer outright. Anything large enough to hold a fall line is 山林 → which is
**legally exactly what you want** (no prior permission, 90-day notification) and unavoidably
unbuilt.

### Price tracks distance from Niseko, not terrain quality

| Category | ¥/ha | MYR/ha |
|---|---|---|
| Raw forest with relief, 30–60 km out | ¥336,000–640,000 | **MYR 8,534–16,256** |
| Forest with a road and a "private ski" story (Rankoshi) | ¥8,000,000 | MYR 203,200 |
| Resort frontage (Rusutsu) | ¥95–136 million | MYR 2.4–3.5 million |

**Buying 30–60 km outside the resort halo is 25–400× cheaper per hectare.** ¥25 M buys 74 ha at
Akaigawa; ¥36 M buys 4.5 ha at Rankoshi. The resort premium buys proximity, not vertical.

## The recommendation that follows: decouple the buildings

The workstream's own conclusion, and it is right:

> **Buildings are the cheap part. The slope is the scarce asset.**
>
> A habitable 5LDK farmhouse on 4,514 m² at Rankoshi is **¥2,500,000 ≈ MYR 63,500**. Turnkey 170 m²
> on 4.5 ha at Makkari is ¥75 M. Meanwhile the only listing in the entire scan marketed as a private
> ski slope delivers **150 m of vertical — half the spec — for ¥36 M**.

**Stop requiring buildings and slope in one title.** Buy the slope as 山林 (¥25–60 M) and a nearby
akiya separately (¥2.5–15 M). The combined cost is far below the bundled products, and the market
does not produce the bundle. This resolves the "nothing qualifies" problem without relaxing any
requirement that matters.

## Correction: Kyōgoku is not dead

`00-terrain-findings.md` recorded that 京極町 sits inside 支笏洞爺国立公園 and concluded the
93.7 ha / ¥60 M parcel was killed. **That was too strong, and this file corrects it.**

The listings workstream obtained the park's zoning breakdown `[P1]`:

| Zone | Area |
|---|---|
| 特別保護地区 | 2,706 ha |
| 特別地域 第1種 | 29,190 ha |
| 特別地域 第2種 | 17,385 ha |
| 特別地域 第3種 | 10,400 ha |
| **普通地域** | **39,792 ha — the largest single zone** |

**Municipality membership does not settle parcel status.** 普通地域 is the least-restricted zone and
is larger than every 特別地域 category combined. A parcel in 普通地域 is a materially different
proposition from one in 特別保護地区.

**Kyōgoku returns to the list as unresolved, not rejected.** Required: the 地番, then the zone.
Details confirmed twice — 虻田郡京極町春日608番1他, 937,359 m², ¥60,000,000 ≈ MYR 1,524,000, agent
株式会社鈴蘭不動産 (licensed, Obihiro, 0155-23-6415).

## Channels that produced nothing, and one that is structurally closed

Searched and empty: 山いちば Hokkaido (all listings 0.2–4.5 ha) · 山林売買.net · 山林バンク ·
municipal akiya banks across 蘭越/留寿都/夕張/芦別/赤井川/島牧 (all sub-0.5 ha residential lots —
structurally the wrong channel for land) · public-pasture disposals (channels exist at
財務省北海道財務局 and 北海道庁財産活用課 but **nothing currently on offer** — this needs periodic
monitoring rather than a one-off search).

**第三者農業経営継承 and BATONZ ranch M&A are closed to this buyer.** A 40+ ha Hokkaido dairy at
¥70 M exists, but these are business transfers requiring 農地所有適格法人 status — not a route for
a non-resident foreign passive investor.

**Closed ski areas are not a channel — this is now checked and settled.** The documented Hokkaido
pattern runs the wrong way: lifts scrapped, **privately-owned slope portions returned to their
original landowners**, buildings and plant given away. No Hokkaido closed ski area was found offered
for freehold sale.

## Reliability caveat

**不動産連合隊, the dominant Hokkaido property portal, was effectively inaccessible** — category
pages indexed, detail pages never surfaced across six attempts. **There is likely qualifying
inventory there that was not seen.** No elevation, aspect, road access, winter clearing, utility,
保安林 or 農用地区域 status could be confirmed for any large parcel from public sources. All of it
requires the tax map, 国土地理院 contours, and direct broker or vendor contact.
