# シャルマン火打 Charmant Hiuchi — full profile

**糸魚川市西飛山, 新潟県 · Nishi-Tobiyama, Itoigawa City, Niigata**

MYR at ¥1 = 0.02540.

---

## ⚠️ Two corrections to this study's earlier reporting

### 1. The Murakami precedent was **not** a transfer of title — and this is the important one

This study reported 村上市ぶどうスキー場 → 株式会社シンクファースト as a **無償譲渡 (free transfer)** and called it *"the working template for the whole play."*

**It was not a title transfer.** In **October 2025** Think First signed a **土地の賃借 — a land lease — plus a three-year 無償貸付 (free loan) of the lifts and lodge. The City kept the land.** `[P2]`

> **That is the actual pattern Niigata municipalities are using, and it is directly adverse to a
> mandate whose absolute condition is owning the slope and its top.** A lease of the pistes with
> a free loan of the plant is an operating concession by another name — the same structure as
> Mt Dobson, which this study rejected outright.

### 2. The ownership chain is one step longer than reported

The City did **not** acquire in April 1998. The area was built by the **former Nou Town (旧能生町)**; land purchase and works ran **1993–97** under the 火打山麓スカイパーク整備事業; the third-sector company was incorporated April 1998 and the hill opened December 1998. **Itoigawa City succeeded to the land by the merger of 19 March 2005** (糸魚川市 + 能生町 + 青海町).

**Title chain: Nou Town → Itoigawa City.** A merger succession, not a purchase — which means the underlying acquisition documents sit in the former town's records.

**Unconfirmed, and each is potentially decisive** `[U]`:

## 2. Zoning — most likely outside the national park, not proven

Itoigawa contains two national parks and three prefectural nature parks.

**妙高戸隠連山国立公園** (designated 27 March 2015, 39,772 ha, split out of 上信越高原国立公園)
covers, on the Itoigawa side, the upland around **雨飾山 and 焼山 — the 早川/根知 side.** The ski
area sits about **20 km away at the head of the 能生川 valley (能生谷)**, topographically separate.
**久比岐県立自然公園 is coastal** (Naoetsu–Nou shoreline).

> **Assessment: outside both — but this is `[E]`, not confirmed.** The official 区域図 could not be
> opened (`env.go.jp/park/content/000105031.pdf`). **That single PDF settles it and should be the
> first check made.**

**One genuine positive `[P1]`:** the prefectural **鉾ヶ岳・権現岳自然環境保全地域** covers parts of
島道, 柵口, 溝尾, 大平 and 土塩 — **西飛山 is not in that list.**

**保安林 (protection forest): 未確認 `[U]`.** No source found. Given a forested headwater catchment
where the Forestry Agency ran **なだれ防止林造成事業 (avalanche-prevention forest works) after
1986**, both 水源かん養 and なだれ防止 designation are live possibilities. **Unresolved, and it
governs whether trails can ever be cut.**

### Aspect — and why a 500 m base works this close to the sea

Every Japanese description says **「日本海を望む北斜面」 — a north-facing slope overlooking the Sea
of Japan.** `[P3]`

> **That answers the aspect question and explains the whole asset.** Sea proximity is normally bad
> for snow quality. Here a **due-north aspect**, plus the 頸城 massif forcing orographic lift
> directly in the **JPCZ convergence band** — hence 3,224 mm of annual precipitation at sea level —
> offsets it. `[E]` **North-facing is the single most favourable terrain fact about this property**,
> and it is why a 500 m base survives to Golden Week.

## 5. Access — a genuine and unusual OPEX win

**新潟県道246号 西飛山能生線** — Niigata **Prefectural** Route 246, 20.2355 km, ~93.6 % paved, running
the left bank of the 能生川. **Managed by Niigata Prefecture through the 糸魚川地域振興局.** `[P1]`

> **Winter clearing of the main access road is the Prefecture's statutory obligation, not the
> landowner's.**
>
> This resolves, in the client's favour, the risk this study identified as *the* small-parcel
> deal-killer — that municipalities do not plough private roads and a non-resident owner gets no
> service. **Here the access is a prefectural road on a prefectural clearing obligation.** Car-park
> and roof snow at a 5 m site remain the owner's problem.

- **~30 minutes from 能生IC** (Hokuriku Expressway)
- **1,000 free parking spaces**

### ⚠️ A 2008 lift accident with governance implications

**December 2008, opening day: a 第1クワッド carrier collision.** The grip closed on a **rope splice
of non-conforming diameter and detached.** The operator had **not measured rope diameter after a
November 2008 haul-rope replacement**, and the **rope-slip detector's fork was not an original
part.** 北陸信越運輸局 investigated and issued a warning (report 7 January 2009). `[P2]`

> **This is not ancient history — it is a documented maintenance-culture failure on plant that is
> now 28 years old.** Any purchase that keeps the lifts running inherits both the equipment and
> the inspection regime. **It is a strong argument for the client's own plan: do not run the
> lifts.**

## 8. ⚠️ Avalanche — the core liability, and it is serious

**26 January 1986: a 泡雪崩 (powder-snow avalanche) released at ~850 m beneath 権現岳 (1,104 m), ran
the east slope for 1,800 m at roughly 180 km/h into 柵口 hamlet. Thirteen people died.** Debris
volume 100,000–300,000 m³, the flow using a depression left by a 1947 landslide. `[P2]`

**Same valley. Same massif. A few kilometres away.**

And: **西飛山 is itself listed among Itoigawa's 地すべり危険箇所 (landslide hazard locations)**, with
a dedicated hazard sheet for **西能生地区５（西飛山地区）**. `[P1]`

> **For a hill whose commercial appeal is 65 % ungroomed terrain and tree runs, this is the central
> risk.** It cuts directly against the client's own terrain rule — buy the hill with no avalanche
> problem — and it is the reason the Forestry Agency's post-1986 avalanche-prevention forest works
> exist in this catchment, which in turn raises the 保安林 question in §2.

## 11. Verdict

**On terrain, aspect, snow and access, this is the best-fitting property in the entire study.**
18.3° mean against a 19° benchmark; a north face; 590 cm of snow; a 138-day season; and a
prefecturally-ploughed access road that solves the small-parcel access problem outright.

**Three things stand between that and a deal:**

1. **The tenure question is unresolved**, and the Murakami precedent suggests Niigata municipalities
   **lease rather than sell.**
2. **The avalanche history is serious and local** — thirteen dead in the next valley, and the site
   itself on the landslide hazard register.
3. **It is four years away**, and gated behind a template that does not yet exist.
