# Search universe: where we looked, and why

The mandate is unusual: it is not "find a ski property" but "find a **large rural landholding
whose own title contains a mountain**". That inverts the normal search. Rather than starting
from ski areas, the search started from **tenure systems** — asking, jurisdiction by
jurisdiction, whether private freehold over genuine alpine terrain exists at all.

## Layer 1 — Jurisdictions screened on tenure availability

| Jurisdiction | Can a foreigner hold freehold over alpine peaks? | Carried forward? |
|---|---|---|
| New Zealand | Yes in principle — but most alpine tops moved to Crown conservation ownership under tenure review, and all rural purchase is screened | **Yes** — client's stated preference |
| Japan | Yes — no nationality bar on freehold today; but high alpine is overwhelmingly 国有林 national forest inside 国立公園 national park | **Yes** — client's stated preference |
| Chile | Yes — broad foreign freehold, large Andean *fundos*, real vertical | **Yes** — strongest "other" |
| Argentina | Restricted — foreign rural land caps plus a 150 km border security zone covering the entire Andes | Screened, low priority |
| United States | Yes for deeded land, but most ski terrain is USFS permit; state-level foreign-farmland laws proliferating | Screened |
| Canada (BC) | Alpine is almost entirely Crown; ski areas hold Controlled Recreation Area tenures | Screened |
| Georgia | Cheap Caucasus alpine, but a constitutional bar on foreign ownership of agricultural land | Screened |
| Kyrgyzstan / Kazakhstan | Foreigners cannot own land — lease only | Screened |
| Türkiye | Foreign purchase permitted subject to reciprocity, a 30 ha per-person cap and military-zone clearance | Screened |
| Romania / Bulgaria / Balkans | Non-EU buyers restricted on agricultural land; company workarounds | Screened |
| Norway | Concession law with residence and operating obligations — effectively closed to a non-resident | Screened |
| Sweden | Open to individuals in much of the country, but all mountain land is encumbered by *renskötselrätt* (Sámi reindeer husbandry rights) | Screened |
| Scotland | No foreign-ownership restriction; large sporting estates with real relief — but marginal snow reliability | Screened |

## Layer 2 — Asset archetypes pursued within the priority jurisdictions

1. **High-country stations / ranches** with freehold reaching the tops — the core target.
2. **Former and closed ski areas** where the underlying land, not just the business, is sold.
3. **Large forest estates** (山林, *predios forestales*) at elevation with road access.
4. **Municipal and state disposals** — public-asset auctions of closed ski fields and
   town-owned uplands, a channel that produces the lowest prices anywhere in the study.
5. **Distressed operating ski businesses** offered through M&A platforms, where the land is
   included.
6. **Small remote settlements / depopulated hamlets** with surrounding upland.

## Layer 3 — Sourcing channels used

- **Brokers:** Bayleys, PGG Wrightson Real Estate, Colliers Rural, NZ Sotheby's, CBRE,
  Property Brokers (NZ); 山いちば, 家いちば, 不動産連合隊, LIFULL/at-home (Japan);
  Portal Inmobiliario, Yapo, Trovit, Nuroa, icasas (Chile); United Country, LandWatch (US).
- **M&A platforms (Japan):** BATONZ バトンズ, TRANBI トランビ, 日本M&Aセンター,
  スピードM&A, M&Aクラウド — the primary channel for distressed ski-area transfers.
- **Government registers and disposals:** LINZ Crown property and pastoral land data, DOC
  concession records (NZ); 財務局 一般競争入札 and prefectural/municipal 未利用財産売却
  pages (Japan); Conservador de Bienes Raíces and SII (Chile).
- **Trade and specialist press:** *Otago Daily Times*, *Farmers Weekly*, OneRoof, RNZ,
  *Tourism Ticker* (NZ); スキー凸凹研究所, Nikkei, Toyo Keizai (Japan); SnowBrains,
  Unofficial Networks, SnowsBest (global ski-asset transactions).
- **Meteorological and topographic data:** NIWA and the NZ Snow and Ice Network; 気象庁 JMA
  最深積雪平年値; 国土地理院 GSI DEM; LINZ DEM; Copernicus GLO-30; Sentinel-2 snow cover.

## Constraint disclosed

The research environment's network policy blocked direct page retrieval from a number of
key sites — including Bayleys, NZ Sotheby's, LINZ, RNZ, Wikipedia, BATONZ and
スキー凸凹研究所 — so those sources are represented through search-engine summaries and
secondary reporting rather than the primary page. Facts sourced this way are graded
accordingly, and the affected items appear in the pre-offer verification list in `05-verification/`.
