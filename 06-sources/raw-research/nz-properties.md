# 0. THE THREE STRUCTURAL FINDINGS THAT GOVERN EVERYTHING BELOW

## 0.1 Tenure review gave the alpine tops to the Crown — and it is now closed
`[INFERENCE from PRIMARY + SECONDARY]` Across the South Island high country, tenure review under the
Crown Pastoral Land Act 1998 (run 1998–2022) systematically **freeholded the lower productive country and
transferred the high-altitude tops to the Crown as conservation land.** Documented instances:

| Station | Freeholded | To Crown/DOC | Source tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earnscleugh (Old Man Range) | ~16,600 ha (two-thirds) | **8,060 ha, "mostly the higher altitude land"** | `[SECONDARY]` Beehive release |
| Godley Peaks | 2,676 ha (376 ha covenanted) | **11,875 ha** incl. 8 ha recreation reserve | `[PRIMARY]` LINZ tenure review |
| Hukarere Station | 5,529 ha (394 ha covenanted) | 1,648 ha | `[PRIMARY]` LINZ tenure review |
| Pisa Range (general) | lower land privatised | "higher pasture retired from farming" | `[SECONDARY]` |
| Old Woman / Old Man (Kopuwai) | — | became conservation land via tenure review + purchase | `[PRIMARY]` DOC |
| Mt Potts (Erewhon Park ski field) | — | ski terrain now under **DOC concession** | `[PRIMARY]` DOC |

**And the door is shut.** The **Crown Pastoral Land Reform Act 2022 ended tenure review** `[SECONDARY]`
(Wolters Kluwer, "End of controversial High Country tenure reviews").
→ `[INFERENCE]` **There is no longer any statutory pathway to convert a pastoral lease over alpine tops into
freehold. What is leasehold today stays leasehold, indefinitely.** Any NZ acquisition must therefore be screened
on the tenure that exists *today*; there is no upside optionality on conversion.

to a very short list.

## 0.3 The Overseas Investment regime is the binding constraint for a Malaysia-based buyer
- **Overseas Investment Act 2005**, s16 — criteria for consent for overseas investments in sensitive land `[PRIMARY]`
- **Overseas Investment (National Interest Test and Other Matters) Amendment Act 2025** — Royal Assent
  **19 Dec 2025**, in force **6 March 2026**; applies to all applications lodged since `[SECONDARY]` White & Case,
  Bell Gully, Russell McVeagh
- The 2025/26 reform introduces a single national interest test and a **fast track — but residential land,
  FARMLAND and fishing quota are expressly CARVED OUT** and remain under the existing, slower, harder screening
  `[SECONDARY]` Russell McVeagh, Bell Gully. **This is the single most important legal fact for this client:
  the liberalisation does not reach the asset class they want.**
- Farmland consent requires **both** the bright-line **investor test** (character/capability) **and the
  "benefit to New Zealand" test — 7 statutory factors**, requiring benefits that would *not* be obtained if the
  buyer were a New Zealander or the property remained in current ownership `[PRIMARY]` LINZ guidance
- **Farmland must first be advertised for sale to New Zealanders on the open market before the consent
  application is made**, under prescribed advertising rules `[PRIMARY]` LINZ
- `[INFERENCE]` A high country station is "sensitive land" on several grounds at once (non-urban >5 ha; adjoining
  conservation land; adjoining lakebed; adjoining reserve). Consent is required **regardless of price.**
- **Crown pastoral lease transfers separately need the consent of the Commissioner of Crown Lands**, who
  **must consider whether the applicant has unreasonably refused public access requests** `[PRIMARY]` LINZ
- → `[INFERENCE]` **A private, access-restricted ski club is directly adverse to both the CCL access test and
  the OIO "benefit to New Zealand" test.** The client's stated end-state is the least consentable use of NZ high
  country by an overseas person. Applications for farmland conversions have been declined for failure to show
  benefit `[SECONDARY]`. This risk should be priced before any NZ offer is made.
- Context: 122 OIO applications 1 Jul 2024 – 19 Jun 2025 (down from 146); LINZ now processes 88% within half the
  statutory timeframe `[SECONDARY]` Beehive
- Active Investor Plus visa holders got a new OIO pathway for high-value **residential** homes (Dec 2025) —
  **does not extend to farmland** `[SECONDARY]` Dentons, Anderson Lloyd

feature of the market, not a sampling artefact of this search.** See §0.1. Tenure review deliberately
freeholded the productive lower country and vested the alpine tops in the Crown, and the
**Crown Pastoral Land Reform Act 2022 closed the process permanently.**
