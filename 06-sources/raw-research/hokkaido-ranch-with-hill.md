# Hokkaido: Ranch/Farm Land With an In-Title Slope (250–400 m Vertical)
## Property-side market scan — what is actually listed for sale

**Researcher scope:** property/listing side only (what is on the market). Terrain/where-the-hills-are analysis is being done separately.
**Date of research:** 2026-08-28. All prices as published on the listing pages/aggregators at that date.
**Method:** 74 Japanese- and English-language web searches. `WebFetch` was blocked at the network egress proxy for every domain attempted (suzuran-fudosan.com, vill.rusutsu.lg.jp, yamaichiba.com, ieichiba.com), so **no listing page could be read directly**. Everything below is from search-engine indexed text and result summaries. Treat all [P3] items as unverified marketing claims.

**Evidence tags:** [P1] government/official · [P2] press · [P3] listing/marketing copy (default; NOT verified fact) · [E] my inference

---

## 1. Headline conclusion

**No property currently on the open market satisfies all three core requirements simultaneously** — (a) ranch/farm character with buildings, (b) a slope *inside the freehold title*, (c) 250–400 m of vertical.

The three attributes are systematically decoupled in the Hokkaido market:

| Attribute cluster | Typical parcel size found | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings + farm/ranch character | **1.5 – 5 ha** | Shibetsu dairy (1.57 ha), Makkari Forest House (4.5 ha), Biei farm-inn (5.0 ha), Kyowa Farm House, Ashibetsu (size unknown) |
| Enough contiguous fall-line slope for 250 m+ vertical | **74 – 365 ha**, but **bare land, no buildings** | Akaigawa 74.4 ha, Kyogoku 93.7 ha, Hamatonbetsu 365 ha |
| Explicitly marketed as a *private ski slope* | **4.5 ha, 150 m vertical** | Rankoshi "Private Mountain" — the only one of its kind found |

**Practical implication:** the realistic route is **buy bare forest land with the vertical, then add buildings** (new build, or separately acquire a cheap adjacent *akiya*). Waiting for a single title that already bundles a farmhouse with 250 m of owned vertical is unlikely to produce a result.

## 3. Notes on each property

### #1 — Akaigawa-mura forest, 74.4 ha, ¥25,000,000 — **top candidate on land, zero on buildings**
- **Verified data points:** listing is on **Jimoty (ジモティー)** in the Hokkaido 山林 category. Stated as **225,000 坪** (= 743,803 m² = 74.38 ha) at **¥25,000,000**. 地目 = **山林**. Seller is a **private individual** (surname given as 小杉/Kosugi in the listing, with a mobile number) who states the land came to them via a relative — **not a licensed broker**, so no 重要事項説明 will be volunteered and no agent-verified survey should be assumed.
- **The listing states property tax is ¥0.** [E] This is a meaningful signal. Two explanations: (a) the assessed value is below the ¥300,000 免税点 (tax-free threshold) for land, or (b) part or all is **保安林 (protection forest)**, which is exempt from fixed-asset tax. Given 74 ha, (b) is the more likely explanation. **For this buyer that is favourable** — 保安林 does not restrict sale or transfer of title and does not prevent skiing over it; it restricts felling and earthworks, and it depresses the price.
- **The "字富田 (aza Tomita)" attribution in the original brief could NOT be confirmed** from any indexed source. Treat the exact 大字/地番 as unknown until the seller supplies the 登記事項証明書.
- **Akaigawa is a caldera.** Village floor ≈ 250 m; the outer rim reaches roughly 700–1,100 m. [E] If this block sits on the rim wall rather than the caldera floor, 300 m of vertical over a fall-line run is geometrically available. If it sits on the floor, it is not. **This single question decides the property.** Ask the seller for the 地番 and check on 国土地理院 / 赤井川村 tax map.
- **Regulatory:** Akaigawa-mura is **not** inside Shikotsu-Toya National Park. [E] The nearest designated park is Niseko-Shakotan-Otaru Kaigan Quasi-National Park, which covers the Niseko range and the coast, not the Akaigawa caldera interior — **so the snowmobile/snowcat prohibition should not apply**, but confirm with 後志総合振興局 保健環境部 環境生活課.
- **地目 山林 means the 農地法 (Agricultural Land Act) barrier does not apply** — this is the single most important legal advantage over any 牧場/畑 property.
- **¥336,000/ha is by far the cheapest ¥/ha of any candidate with plausible relief.**
- **Missing:** buildings, road access, winter snow clearing, electricity, water, exact boundary, 保安林 confirmation, whether inside 農用地区域 (unlikely for 山林 but confirm).
- Source: https://jmty.jp/hokkaido/est-kw-%E5%B1%B1%E6%9E%97

### #2 — Kyogoku-cho Kasuga 608-1, 93.74 ha, ¥60,000,000 — **largest broker-listed parcel; park status is the gate**
- **Confirmed independently by two searches:** 虻田郡京極町春日６０８番１他, **937,359 m²**, **¥60,000,000**, listed by **株式会社鈴蘭不動産 (Suzuran Fudosan)**, a licensed broker based in Obihiro (西一条南19丁目, tel 0155-23-6415). It sits in their 山林・郊外 category, which holds 13 listings.
- 地目 = 山林. **¥640,000/ha.** No buildings mentioned.
- **National park status is unresolved and is decisive.** [P1] Kyogoku-cho "extends along the eastern foothills of Mt Yotei" and is one of the constituent municipalities of Shikotsu-Toya National Park. The park is zoned: 特別保護地区 2,706 ha / 特別地域 1種 29,190 ha / 2種 17,385 ha / 3種 10,400 ha / 普通地域 39,792 ha, and **Mt Yotei itself is designated 特別保護地区**. If Kasuga 608 lies within a 特別地域 or 特別保護地区, **snowmobiles and snowcats are prohibited even on private land** (up to 1 year imprisonment or ¥1,000,000 fine) — though skiing and snowshoeing remain unregulated. **Get the exact 地番 and run it against the 環境省 park zoning map / 後志総合振興局.**
- **Missing:** elevation range, aspect, park zoning, 保安林 status, road access, utilities, buildings.
- Sources: https://suzuran-fudosan.com/bukken/sanrin-kougai?so=kak&ord=d · https://www.shiribeshi.pref.hokkaido.lg.jp/hk/kks/parks.html · https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/支笏洞爺国立公園

### #3 — Rankoshi "Private Mountain", 4.5 ha, ¥36,000,000 — **exactly the right concept, half the required vertical**
- This is the **only listing found in the whole scan that is explicitly marketed as a private ski slope**. Listing copy: "almost 4.5 hectares... runs from the base up to the ridge of a small mountain. Build your own lodge at the base and enjoy the mountain with **150 m of vertical drop and about 500 m of run**. There is a road all the way to the top of the ridge, giving you direct access to your very own private off-piste run."
- **¥36,000,000 (~US$230,000).** 5 min from 目名 (Mena), 10 min from Rankoshi town, 30 min drive to Niseko United / Moiwa.
- **Why it falls short:** 150 m vertical vs the 250–400 m target (Jixue reference: 296 m). 500 m run vs the 871 m needed for 300 m at 19°. 4.5 ha vs the ~35 ha needed for a 400 m-wide, 871 m-long fall-line envelope. **No buildings.**
- **Why it still matters:** it proves this product type exists and is brokered in English at a price this buyer can absorb, and it establishes a market comp of **¥8,000,000/ha for slope-with-a-road** vs **¥336,000/ha for raw forest** — i.e. the "ski-ready" premium is roughly 24×.
- Sources: https://uchijapan.com/properties/rankoshi-private-mountain · https://habitathokkaido.com/property-type/land/

### #10 — Rankoshi akiya bank #24-9, ¥2,500,000 — **confirmed, but disqualified on size**
- [P1] Confirmed via Rankoshi town akiya bank: **¥2,500,000**, 5LDK, **94.21 m²**, built **1969 (昭和44)**, land = 宅地 907.23 m² + 山林 1,004 m² + 山林 1,487 m² + **畑 1,116 m²** = **4,514.23 m² (0.4514 ha)**.

It would have to be excluded from the sale or converted via 非農地証明, which the town/agricultural committee controls.
- The brief's own thesis — "the key is whether adjacent forest can be added" — remains the only way this works, and that is a private negotiation with unknown neighbours, not a listing.

### #16–17 — BATONZ M&A listings — **legally the wrong door**
- A Hokkaido dairy business **holding 40+ ha**, revenue ¥100–200 M, **asking ¥70,000,000** (updated 2025-06-04); and a "high-productivity agricultural corporation with broad landholdings" at **¥38,000,000** (updated 2026-02-18).
- **[E] Not a viable route for this buyer.** These are business/share transfers. Owning farmland through a company requires 農地所有適格法人 status (majority of voting rights held by persons engaged in the farming business, etc.). A non-resident foreign passive investor cannot satisfy this. Same objection applies to the 北海道農業担い手育成センター 第三者農業経営継承 (third-party farm succession) channel — it requires the successor to actually become a farmer.
- Sources: https://batonz.jp/sell_cases/pref_1/?q=牧場 · https://www.adhokkaido.or.jp/ninaite/transfer/

## 5. Cross-cutting observations

1. **The 地目 filter is doing most of the work.** Every property that has ranch character has 畑 or 田 in the title breakdown (Rankoshi #24-9, Biei, Kyowa), which puts it out of reach under 農地法 for a non-resident foreigner. Every property with the size to hold real vertical is 地目 山林 — which is exactly the category the brief prioritises, and which is also, unavoidably, unbuilt.
2. **Price stratification is extreme and tracks proximity to Niseko/Rusutsu, not terrain quality.** Raw forest with relief: ¥336,000–¥640,000/ha (Akaigawa, Kyogoku). Forest with a road and a ski story: ¥8,000,000/ha (Rankoshi). Resort-frontage land: ¥95,000,000–¥136,000,000/ha (Rusutsu golf, Niseko). **Buying terrain 30–60 km outside the resort halo is 25–400× cheaper per hectare.** Akaigawa and Kyogoku are the two places where the client can buy 74–94 ha for less than a single Niseko building plot.
3. **¥25 M for 74 ha (Akaigawa) vs ¥36 M for 4.5 ha (Rankoshi Private Mountain)** — for less money the client gets 16× the land, at the cost of having to establish the vertical themselves rather than having it advertised.
4. **The ¥0 property tax on the Akaigawa parcel is a feature, not a bug** under the client's own stated criteria: it points to 保安林, which is freely transferable, restricts only felling and earthworks, does not impede skiing, and suppresses the price.
5. **Buildings are the cheap part of this problem.** A finished 170 m² 4BR house on 4.5 ha is ¥75 M (Makkari); a habitable 5LDK is ¥2.5 M (Rankoshi akiya bank); a derelict farmhouse with barns is low single-digit millions. **The slope is the scarce asset; the buildings can be bought or built afterwards.** The brief's insistence on buildings being present in the same title is the constraint most worth relaxing.

## 6. Gaps in this scan (be aware before acting)

- **`WebFetch` was blocked at the network proxy for every domain**, so **not one listing page was read directly**. All figures come from search-engine index text. Every [P3] number below the top three should be re-confirmed against the live page.
- **不動産連合隊 (the dominant Hokkaido MLS-style portal) was effectively inaccessible.** Its category pages index but its listing detail pages did not surface in any of 6 attempts. There is likely qualifying inventory there that this scan did not see.
- **Prices not obtained:** Ashibetsu #7 (also area), Kyowa #8 (also area), Rankoshi Big Land #4, Rusutsu Route 230 #6, Shibetsu #9.
- **The Akaigawa parcel's 大字/地番 is unconfirmed.** The "字富田" in the original brief could not be corroborated.
- **The Kyogoku parcel's national park zoning is unconfirmed** and is the single decisive fact about it.
- **Elevation ranges, aspects, road access, snow clearing, and utilities were not obtainable for any of the large parcels** from listing text. These require the tax map, 国土地理院 contours, and direct seller/broker contact.
- **農用地区域 (blue-zone) status was not confirmable for any property** from public web sources — it requires the municipality's 農業振興地域整備計画 map. For 地目 山林 parcels it is unlikely but must be checked.
