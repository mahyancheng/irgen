## 1. Land tenure (土地の所有形態) — the decisive question

### What is established
- The ski area was created by the **former Nou Town (旧能生町 / kyū-Nō-machi)**, not by Itoigawa City. Planning began 1992 as a response to youth outflow and depopulation; **land acquisition and construction ran 1993–1997** under the **「火打山麓スカイパーク整備事業」 (Hiuchi Sanroku Sky Park Development Project)**. [P3]
- The third-sector operating company **火打山麓振興株式会社 (Hiuchi Sanroku Shinkō K.K. — "Hiuchi Foothills Development Co., Ltd.")** was incorporated **April 1998**; the ski area opened **December 1998**. [P3]
- **Correction to the buyer's brief:** Itoigawa City did not acquire the land in April 1998. Nou Town owned it; **Itoigawa City succeeded to it by the 2005-03-19 municipal merger** of 糸魚川市 + 西頸城郡能生町 (Nou-machi) + 青海町 (Ōmi-machi). [P3] Any title chain runs Nou Town → Itoigawa City.
- Both press outlets covering the December 2025 committee describe the assets as **市が所有する / 市所有の2スキー場** — "the two ski areas owned by the City" (Seaside Valley at 山口 / Yamaguchi, and Charmant Hiuchi at 西飛山 / Nishi-Tobiyama). [P2 新潟日報 Niigata Nippo; P2 上越タイムス Joetsu Times]
- The City publishes the management results of third-sector entities in which it holds **50 % or more equity**; 火打山麓振興 appears on that page → **the City holds ≥50 %** of the operator. [P1 city page 「第3セクターの経営状況」]

### What is NOT established (未確認 — must be obtained before any offer)
1. **Whether the City holds fee simple over the entire ski-area footprint.** No cadastral, ledger or registry evidence was retrievable.
2. **National-forest lease (国有林貸付 / kokuyūrin kashitsuke).** No evidence found either way. Forestry Agency national-forest land is extensively leased for ski areas nationwide; the absence of a search hit is *not* evidence of absence. Must be checked with 中部森林管理局 / 上越森林管理署.
3. **Property ward land (財産区 / zaisanku) or common forest (共有林 / kyōyūrin).** Not found. In the 能生谷 (Nou-dani) valley, hamlet common forest is a realistic possibility for upper slopes.
4. **Administrative vs ordinary property classification (行政財産 / 普通財産).** Unconfirmed. This is procedurally decisive: 行政財産 cannot be sold until 用途廃止 (deallocation) is resolved by council; only 普通財産 can be disposed of.
5. **Acquisition area and price** paid by Nou Town 1993–97; total project cost.
6. **Ordinance** — a facility-establishment ordinance (設置及び管理に関する条例) almost certainly exists and would state the facility name, location and designated-manager clause; not retrieved.

## 2. Natural-park zoning (自然公園法の地種区分)

### Established
- Itoigawa City contains **two national parks** — 中部山岳国立公園 (Chūbu-Sangaku NP) and **妙高戸隠連山国立公園 (Myōkō-Togakushi-renzan National Park)** — and **three prefectural natural parks** — 親不知・子不知県立自然公園, **久比岐県立自然公園 (Kubiki Prefectural Natural Park)**, 白馬山麓県立自然公園. [P1 city]
- 妙高戸隠連山 NP: designated **2015-03-27** as Japan's 32nd national park, carved out of 上信越高原国立公園 (Jōshin'etsu-kōgen NP). Area **39,772 ha**, 2 prefectures / 6 municipalities (Itoigawa, Myōkō, Nagano City, Otari, Shinano, Iizuna). Contains 焼山 (Yakeyama), 火打山 (Hiuchiyama, 2,462 m), 雨飾山 (Amakazariyama), 妙高山, 戸隠山. In the **Itoigawa portion the park covers the upland around 雨飾山 and 焼山** (鎌池, hot springs). [P1 環境省 / 糸魚川市]
- **久比岐県立自然公園** is a coastal park: the shoreline from 直江津 (Naoetsu) to 能生 (Nou), plus 春日山城 and 能生白山神社. [P1 新潟県]
- **鉾ヶ岳・権現岳自然環境保全地域 (Hokogatake–Gongendake Natural Environment Conservation Area)** — a Niigata Prefecture designation, *separate from the Natural Parks Act*. Main peak 鉾ヶ岳 1,316 m, with 権現岳 1,106 m and 大沢岳 1,242 m forming a steep コ-shaped massif. **Designated over parts of 糸魚川市大字島道 (Shimamichi), 柵口 (Sekiguchi), 溝尾 (Mizoo), 大平 (Ōdaira), 土塩 (Tsuchishio)**. Protects *Rhododendron degronianum* (ホンシャクナゲ) above ~750 m at its natural northern limit. [P1 新潟県]

### Assessment
- **西飛山 (Nishi-Tobiyama) does not appear in the list of ōaza covered by the 鉾ヶ岳・権現岳 conservation area** → the ski area is most likely outside it. [P1 + E]
- The ski area sits at 500–1,009 m at the head of the 能生川 (Nou-gawa) valley, roughly 20 km inland and topographically separate from the 焼山/火打山/雨飾山 massif that forms the Itoigawa slice of the national park. **My working assessment is that the ski area lies OUTSIDE 妙高戸隠連山国立公園 and outside 久比岐県立自然公園** — but **this is [E], not confirmed.** [E]
- **Required verification:** the official zoning map, 「妙高戸隠連山国立公園 区域及び公園計画図（その1）」, `https://www.env.go.jp/park/content/000105031.pdf`, plus the e-Gov dataset `env_20161031_0014`, and the prefectural GIS 「にいがたeマップ」 / 「eまっぷいといがわ」 (itoigawa.geogeo.jp). If any part is inside a 特別地域 (Special Zone), tree felling, earth-moving, structures and even piste widening become permit-triggering under 自然公園法 §20; a 第1種特別地域 or 特別保護地区 would effectively freeze development.
- **Protection forest (保安林 / hoanrin): 未確認.** No source located. Given a forested headwater catchment in an extreme-snow zone, **水源かん養保安林 (water-source conservation)** and/or **なだれ防止保安林 (avalanche-prevention)** designation is a live possibility — note that Forestry Agency avalanche-prevention forest works (なだれ防止林造成事業) were carried out in this very valley after 1986 (§10). Check with 新潟県 農林水産部 治山課 and 糸魚川地域振興局.

### Aspect — the key snow-retention fact
Every Japanese description of the resort uses the same formula: **「火打山の麓にレイアウトされた日本海を望む北斜面」** — *"laid out on the foothills of Mt. Hiuchi, a **north-facing slope** overlooking the Sea of Japan."* [P3, repeated across にいがた観光ナビ, tenki.jp, SURF&SNOW, Snow Paradise Tōhoku]

**The principal slope aspect is NORTH.** For a base at only 500 m this is the single most valuable physical attribute: north aspect at this latitude (37°05′N) keeps direct insolation off the snowpack and is the reason the area can run to Golden Week (early May) despite a low base.

### Named runs identified
- **ヴォレー (Volley)** — longest groomed run, 1,623 m.
- **プリンセス (Princess)** — ungroomed, 780 m, max 32°.
- Remaining 14 course names, individual lengths, gradients and per-course aspects: **未確認**.
- **Summit/ridge toponym at 1,009 m: 未確認.** It was not identified. The resort is at the head of 能生谷 adjacent to the 鉾ヶ岳 (1,316 m) – 大沢岳 (1,242 m) – 権現岳 (1,106 m) massif, but no source placed the 1,009 m top on a named peak. Resolve from GSI 1:25,000 (地理院地図).

### Why the snow is good — assessment [E]
No source states a technical reason; the "best snow quality in Japan" line is marketing. The physically defensible explanation is: (a) **north aspect**; (b) position ~15–20 km from the Sea of Japan directly in the path of the winter NW monsoon and the Japan-Sea Polar-air Convergence Zone (JPCZ), which is what drives the 能生 3,224 mm annual precipitation; (c) the 頸城 (Kubiki) mountain wall forcing orographic lift immediately inland; (d) a 500–1,009 m band that is cold enough in Jan–Feb for dry snow while low enough to catch the full convergence band. Being close to the sea is normally a *negative* for snow quality; here the north aspect and the immediate mountain rise offset it. **This is inference, not a sourced claim.**

## 5. Lifts and ropeway (索道) assets

### Established
- **3 lifts: 1 × detachable/fixed quad ("第1クワッドリフト") + 2 × pair lifts.** [P3, consistently]
- All are presumed original to the December 1998 opening → **~28 years old in the 2026-27 season**. [E — installation years not individually confirmed]
- The **operator (火打山麓振興株式会社), not the City, is the licensed ropeway undertaking (索道事業者)** under the 鉄道事業法. It publishes an annual **索道安全報告書 (Ropeway Safety Report)** at `charmant-hiuchi.jp/report/`; an FY2021 (令和3年度) edition is confirmed to exist. [P1-equivalent operator disclosure]
  - **Consequence for the buyer:** the ropeway licence sits with the third-sector company. Acquiring the *land* does not automatically convey the right to operate the lifts; either the company is acquired, or a fresh 索道事業許可 must be obtained from 北陸信越運輸局 (Hokuriku-Shin'etsu District Transport Bureau).

### Safety history — material finding
**December 2008 (season-opening day): 第1クワッドリフト 搬器衝突事故 — carrier collision on the No. 1 quad.** [P1/P2]
- The grip of carrier No. 32 closed on a **rope splice** where the rope diameter exceeded the standard value and the cross-section was irregular; the grip detached from the haul rope.
- At tower No. 6 (the last pressure sheave on the mountain-top side) the "tang" (a synthetic-resin auxiliary device that eases the grip past pressure sheaves) caught on the inner anti-derailment plate on the left of the direction of travel.
- Contributing causes: the operator **did not measure rope diameter or verify conformity to standard** after the haul rope was replaced by a maintenance contractor in **November 2008**; and the detection fork of the rope-slip detector that failed to actuate **was not an original part**.
- 北陸信越運輸局 despatched 3 officials on 28 December; the **「シャルマン火打第１クワッドリフト搬器衝突事故検討会」** (investigation panel) report was received **2009-01-07**. The Bureau issued a **warning** to the operator.

This is a governance red flag, not merely a technical one: it evidences a period of inadequate ropeway maintenance verification. Demand the full 索道安全報告書 series, the 定期検査記録, haul-rope replacement history and NDT records.

### 未確認
- Individual lift lengths, vertical, capacity (persons/hour), manufacturer, installation year, most recent major overhaul.
- Remaining useful life and replacement cost.
- **Demolition/removal cost if a lift is retired** — no reliable Japanese benchmark was obtained; do not assume a figure. This is a real contingent liability and should be quoted by a 索道 contractor (e.g. 日本ケーブル, 安全索道) during diligence.

## 9. Transfer process

### The decision on record
**2025-12-15, 糸魚川市議会 建設産業常任委員会 (Construction & Industry Standing Committee):** the City presented its policy for transferring both designated-managed ski areas to private operators. [P2 上越タイムス, P2 新潟日報]

- **糸魚川シーサイドバレースキー場 (山口):** concrete procedures from **FY2026**, targeting completion **by FY2028**; the designated-management term from FY2026 is shortened to a **1-year renewal**; **public solicitation (公募) procedures begin FY2026**.
- **シャルマン火打スキー場 (西飛山): private transfer targeted for FY2029 (令和11年度) onward, to be deliberated during the FY2026–FY2028 (令和8–10年度) three-year designated-management term.**

Stated issues driving the decision: public cost burden via designated-management fees; declining ski population; green-season utilisation.

### Foreign-investor eligibility — the most important finding in this section
Regarding the identity of the private transferee, the City's **商工観光課 (Commerce & Tourism Division)** stated:
> **「海外資本の企業も視野に入れたい考えもある」**
> *"There is also a view that we would like to keep foreign-capital companies within our field of vision."*
[P2 上越タイムス, 2025-12-15 committee, stated in the Seaside Valley context]

This is an explicit, on-the-record municipal statement that **foreign-capital enterprises are contemplated as acceptable transferees.** No nationality restriction on applicants was found anywhere. Caveat: it is a division-level remark reported in the press, not a published eligibility rule; the actual 公募要項 (solicitation guidelines) will govern.

### 未確認
- **Whether the transfer will be 無償 (free) or 有償 (for consideration).** No policy statement found. Unresolved.
- **Whether it will transfer ownership at all, or only a lease / free loan of facilities.** See the precedent below — this distinction is doing a lot of work.
- The ski areas' treatment in the **糸魚川市公共施設等総合管理指針 / 個別計画**. The City does publish category-level individual plans (a スポーツ施設 individual plan PDF exists at `city.itoigawa.lg.jp/uploaded/attachment/8334.pdf`), but the ski-area entry was not retrieved.
- Whether 用途廃止 (deallocation from administrative property) is contemplated, and on what timetable.

### Precedent 1 — 村上市ぶどうスキー場 (Murakami City Budō Ski Area) — **correction to the brief's premise**
- Opened FY1988 by the former 朝日村 (Asahi-mura); **closed March 2025** on declining use and ageing plant.
- **シンクファースト (Think First, Tokyo — an IT company; president 沼前純一 / Numamae Jun'ichi)** stepped in. The president first visited the resort on its final operating day and then set up a resort business to keep it alive.
- **In October 2025 the company signed a contract with Murakami City to LEASE THE LAND (土地の賃借) and to BORROW THE LIFTS, LODGE AND OTHER FACILITIES FREE OF CHARGE FOR THREE YEARS (無償貸し付け / 3年間無償で借り受ける).** Reopened as **ぶどうスノーリゾート**, targeting a 20 December opening; year-round operation via campsite and a new RV park is envisaged. [P2 新潟日報 / NST / UX / FNN]

**This was NOT a freehold transfer.** It was a land lease plus a three-year free loan of improvements. For a buyer whose absolute requirement is ownership of the slopes and the summit, this precedent points the wrong way: the pattern Niigata municipalities are actually using is *lease + free loan*, which preserves municipal title and leaves the operator with no asset and a renewal cliff.

## 10. Risk register

### 10.1 Avalanche — the dominant physical risk in this valley
**柵口雪崩災害 (Sekiguchi Avalanche Disaster), 1986-01-26** [P1/P2 — Wikipedia, 日本雪工学会 and 雪氷 (Seppyō) journal papers]
- A **泡雪崩 (awa-nadare — powder/airborne avalanche)** released from about **850 m elevation, directly beneath the summit of 権現岳 (Gongendake, 1,104 m)**, ran the **east slope**, and struck the **柵口 (Sekiguchi)** hamlet at the foot: **11 households, 36 people hit, 13 killed.**
- Estimated **maximum speed 180 km/h, runout distance 1,800 m, debris volume 100,000–300,000 m³.**
- The release channel exploited a topographic depression created by the **1947 (昭和22) 柵口地すべり (Sekiguchi landslide)** — i.e. slide path and landslide terrain are coupled here.
- Response works, still in place: Ministry of Construction avalanche countermeasure works; Forestry Agency 災害関連緊急治山事業 and **なだれ防止林造成事業 (avalanche-prevention forest creation)**; **雪崩導流堤 (deflection dyke), 雪崩誘導堤 (guide dyke), 減勢柵 (energy-dissipating fence), 防護柵 (protection fence)**.

柵口 is in the same 能生谷 valley as the resort, a few kilometres away, on the same massif. **This is the reference hazard for the terrain the buyer would own.** A resort marketed on 65 % ungroomed terrain and tree runs in a valley with a 13-fatality powder-avalanche history carries real operational liability.

**未確認:** the resort's own avalanche-control programme (explosive control, closures, patrol structure), whether beacon-mandatory zones exist, and whether there has been any avalanche incident inside the ski-area boundary. The resort publicises tree running and open-forest policy but no safety-management detail was retrievable.

### 10.2 Landslide / sediment disaster
- **西飛山 (Nishi-Tobiyama) is listed among Itoigawa City's 地すべり危険箇所 (landslide danger locations).** [P1 新潟県 disaster-prevention plan resource]
- Itoigawa City has **58 landslide danger locations under Forestry Agency jurisdiction** (as at end-FY2022). [P1 新潟県]
- The City publishes a 土砂災害ハザードマップ sheet for **西能生地区５（西飛山地区）** — i.e. the resort's own hamlet has a dedicated hazard sheet. [P1 糸魚川市]
- A 2012 social-media report referenced landslide risk near the access road. [P3, low reliability, uncorroborated]
- **未確認:** whether 土砂災害警戒区域 (yellow zone) or 土砂災害特別警戒区域 (red zone) polygons overlie the ski area, buildings, or the access road. Check 新潟県土木部砂防課 (`sabou-niigata.jp/dosyahou/`) and 「eまっぷいといがわ」 (`itoigawa.geogeo.jp/maps`). A red zone over the centre house would constrain rebuilding.

### 10.3 Ropeway
See §5 — the December 2008 quad carrier-collision accident and the maintenance-verification failures behind it. Contingent CAPEX for lift renewal within the holding period should be assumed, not hoped away.

### 10.4 Important Land Survey Act (重要土地等調査法)
The Cabinet Office's Niigata Prefecture designation list (per 令和6年内閣府告示第91号 area maps) covers **新潟市, 柏崎市, 新発田市, 上越市, 佐渡市, 刈羽郡刈羽村**. **糸魚川市 is not on that list.** [P1 内閣府]

→ **The site appears NOT to fall in a 注視区域 (watch zone) or 特別注視区域 (special watch zone).** Consequently the Act's pre-transaction notification obligation for a foreign buyer would not be triggered. **Verify at `resum2.go.jp` (the official designated-area viewer) before relying on this.** Note that designations are periodically extended; re-check at signing.

### 10.5 Other
- **2016 糸魚川大火 (Itoigawa great fire, Dec 2016):** an urban conflagration in the Itoigawa town centre, roughly 20 km from the resort. **No relevance to the site.** Correctly excluded.
- **Volcanic:** 焼山 (Yakeyama, 2,400 m) is an active volcano roughly 20 km ESE with an active eruption-alert regime. Whether the resort falls in any 火山災害警戒地域 designation is **未確認**; distance makes direct exposure unlikely [E], but ashfall on a snowpack is a plausible seasonal nuisance.
- **Heavy snow itself:** roof snow loads, building structural certification, and the standing cost of keeping a 1,000-space car park and access spur open at a site that regularly exceeds 5 m of settled snow.
