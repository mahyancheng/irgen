evidence trail behind the curated briefs. Each contains its own **source register** (URL plus

verify.

## Reading these files

They are **working notes, not the deliverable.** They are franker than the briefs about what
failed, and several open with an explicit statement that the analyst could not complete the
assigned scope. That is deliberate and should be read as a feature: **the gaps are recorded
rather than filled with plausible-sounding invention.**

Where a raw file and a curated brief disagree, the brief is the considered position — but check
the raw file's tag before relying on any number.
