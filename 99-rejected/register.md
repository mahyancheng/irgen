# Rejected register

Recording what was screened out and why. A rejection here is a finding, not an omission —
several of these are the most instructive results in the study.

## Jurisdictions rejected on exclusivity — you can own it but not control it

| Jurisdiction | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Sweden** | ***Renskötselrätt*** — the Sámi herding right is a constitutionally-protected property right by *urminnes hävd* that **burdens private freehold, survives sale, and cannot be excluded** across the whole fjäll west of the *odlingsgränsen*. Snowcats and pistes are the archetypal conflicting use. Plus *allemansrätten* |
| **Scotland** | **Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003** gives statutory public access over essentially all land. A private mountain is not legally possible — and the Land Reform Bill's proposed "lotting" regime could force this asset class to be broken up on sale |
| **Norway** | *Konsesjon* + *boplikt* (residence obligation) + *driveplikt* + *priskontroll* — a regime designed to exclude non-residents |

## Rejected on regime instability

| Jurisdiction | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Argentina** | The *Ley de Tierras Rurales* was under **active renegotiation in August 2026** with a proposed 25 % foreign cap. Separately, **Decreto-Ley 15.385/44's frontier security zone (~150 km)** requires prior State consent for foreign acquirers and covers essentially the entire Argentine Andes. If current, close to disqualifying on its own — **one search would close this question** |
| **Georgia** | Constitutional bar on foreign ownership of agricultural land (2018), plus severe 2024–26 rule-of-law deterioration |
| **Bosnia / Montenegro / N. Macedonia / Albania / Kosovo** | Cadastre and restitution disorder makes it impossible to assemble contiguous scale with clean title |
