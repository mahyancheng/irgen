## Why the FX basis matters more than usual here

This is a **multi-decade, unhedged, foreign-currency land position funded in MYR**. Three
consequences follow, and they are not academic:

1. **The entry price in MYR is a currency bet as much as a land bet.** A 15 % move in NZD/MYR
   over a purchase timeline — entirely ordinary — moves a NZ$8 m purchase by roughly RM 2.9 m,
   which is larger than the entire Phase 1 ski CAPEX in most scenarios.
2. **Carrying costs are incurred in the local currency, forever, against MYR income.** The
   carrying-cost projections in `04-economics/` should be read as MYR-equivalent at today's
   rate, not as a fixed MYR obligation.
3. **Malaysian outward-investment rules apply.** Bank Negara Malaysia's Foreign Exchange
   Policy notices place conditions on investment abroad by residents, with the treatment
   depending on whether the investor has domestic ringgit borrowing. This must be confirmed
   with the client's Malaysian bank **before** an offer, not after — it can constrain both
   size and timing. Flagged for verification.

Rates should be re-struck at the date of any offer, and a forward or staged conversion
considered for the deposit-to-settlement window.
