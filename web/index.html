Rim peaks run 元服山 → 大黒山 → 丸山 → 石山, north then counter-clockwise. 丸山 and 石山 sit on the south and south-west rim, whose inner faces look north to north-east — the aspects that hold snow. Neither elevation could be found on the open web. Switch to 標準地図 and read them off the map.

Specification

Target 22–28° average over a band delivering ~300 m of vertical. Sustained pitches ceiling 30° — above that the dry-slab avalanche band opens (30–45°, peak 35–38°) and a conventional snowcat stops working (~25°). Short benched pitches to 38° are acceptable; long continuous 33–40° faces are not.
